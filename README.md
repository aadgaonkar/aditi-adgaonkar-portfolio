# About me
Hi, I'm Aditi Adgaonkar. I'm finishing my Master of Information Systems at Carnegie Mellon University (December 2026). I'm a product manager at heart, and I like working where data, AI and real users meet.

Before CMU, I was a Technical Analyst at Ion Trading Group in Pune, India, where I worked on an enterprise financial risk platform used by Fortune 500 clients. I turned customer workflows and SQL data into product requirements and KPIs. This past summer I was a Product Manager Intern at Cloudflare, where I built an internal AI assistant for employee support workflows and a framework for evaluating how well it worked.

At CMU I'm also a graduate teaching assistant in the School of Computer Science (Distributed Systems and Product Management Essentials), and I mentor students with Women in Product at CMU. I did my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Engineering at Vellore Institute of Technology in India.

# What I hope to learn
I make decisions from dashboards, logs and metrics, but a correct number doesn't help if the person reading it can't understand it. In this course I hope to:

- Choose the right chart for the question, and use color, scale and labeling on purpose instead of by default.
- Turn analysis into a clear story that product, engineering and executive audiences can act on.
- Get better at critiquing graphics, mine and other people's, with specific and constructive feedback.
- Build a portfolio I can show to future employers.

After graduating, I plan to work as a product manager on data-driven and AI-powered products.
