About Me

Hi, I'm Aditi Adgaonkar. I'm finishing my Master of Information Systems at Carnegie Mellon University (December 2026). I'm a product manager at heart, and I like working where data, AI, and real users meet.

Before CMU, I was a Technical Analyst at ION Trading Group in Pune, India, where I worked on an enterprise financial risk platform used by Fortune 500 clients. I turned customer workflows and SQL data into product requirements and KPIs.

This past summer, I was a Product Manager Intern at Cloudflare, where I built an internal AI assistant for employee support workflows and a framework for evaluating how well it worked.

At CMU, I'm also a graduate teaching assistant in the School of Computer Science (Distributed Systems and Product Management Essentials), and I mentor students with Women in Product at CMU. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Engineering at Vellore Institute of Technology in India.

What I Hope to Learn

I make decisions from dashboards, logs, and metrics, but a correct number doesn't help if the person reading it can't understand it.

In this course, I hope to:

  • Choose the right chart for the question, and use color, scale, and labeling on purpose.
  • Turn analysis into a clear story that product, engineering, and executive audiences can act on.
  • Get better at critiquing graphics, mine and other people's, with specific and constructive feedback.
  • Build a portfolio I can show future employers.

After graduating, I plan to work as a product manager on data-driven and AI-powered products.
